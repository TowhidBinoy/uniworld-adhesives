Technical & B2B FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick technical answers on bond strength, spray viscosity, primers, pack sizes, and nationwide delivery across Bangladesh.

Explore All 14 FAQs Compare All 15 Grades
Premium Polyurethane (PU) Adhesive delivers our highest peel strength (up to 5.2 N/mm / 23.0 kgf 24 hours after bonding) with tropical heat resistance up to 85°C, followed by Standard PU Adhesive (18.5 kgf).
SBS Sprayable Adhesive (Clear and White SB602). At 80–120 cPs viscosity, it is the lowest-viscosity grade in our range—engineered for clean spray atomization and soft seams on PU foam, mattresses, and upholstery.
Low-surface-energy soles like EVA and Phylon require EVA Primer after cleaning with Synthetic Cleaner so the adhesive anchors properly. Molded PU, PVC, and TPR soles benefit from PU/PVC Primer to treat plasticizers and prevent sole separation.
Acetic Silicone Sealant cures fast for glass, ceramic tiles, and sanitary fixtures, while Neutral Cure Silicone Sealant is low-odor and non-corrosive—preventing mirror desilvering and metal oxidation.
Liquid adhesives, primers, and cleaners come in 3 L tins, 15 L industrial pails, and 200 L drums, while sealants come in 300 ml cartridges. Factories can request trial samples and TDS/SDS sheets directly via our Contact & Quote Desk.
