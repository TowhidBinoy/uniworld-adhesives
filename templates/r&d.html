Surface Science Proof

How Uniworld Surface Prep Multiplies Bond Strength

Molded EVA and PU shoe soles carry invisible silicone release agents. Our lab data demonstrates the peel-strength jump when pairing Synthetic Cleaner and EVA/PU Primers with Uniworld adhesives:

UNPRIMED SURFACE

Adhesive Only (No Prep)

1.4 N/mm

Residual mould-release wax blocks polymer wetting, risking edge delamination under flex stress.

SOLVENT WIPED

Synthetic Cleaner + Adhesive

2.9 N/mm

Wiping with Synthetic Cleaner lifts surface grease and release oils for clean contact.

FULL LAB SYSTEM

Cleaner + Primer + PU / Graft CR

5.2 N/mm

EVA Primer or PU/PVC Primer chemically activates the substrate for substrate-tearing bond strength.
