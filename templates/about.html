Company Overview

Who We Are

Founded in 2018, Uniworld Adhesives & Sealants is a Bangladesh-based manufacturer of industrial adhesives, sealants, and bonding solutions. From our head office in Dhaka and manufacturing facility in Rupganj, Narayanganj, we serve businesses across a range of industries with products developed to meet the demands of modern manufacturing.

01 · Product Range & Formulations

Our product range includes SBS sprayable adhesives, synthetic rubber adhesives, polyurethane adhesives, graft adhesives, primers, silicone sealants, and industrial cleaning solutions. Each product is developed with its intended application in mind, helping manufacturers achieve dependable bonding, consistent production quality, and lasting performance across a variety of materials and working conditions.

02 · ISO 9001:2015 Quality & Reliability

As an ISO 9001:2015 certified company, we place strong emphasis on quality management, manufacturing consistency, and continuous improvement. Our production processes are supported by quality control measures designed to maintain product reliability and meet the requirements of our customers. We combine practical industry knowledge with ongoing product development to address the evolving needs of the industries we serve.

03 · Industry Partnership & Long-Term Growth

At Uniworld, our role extends beyond manufacturing and supplying products. We work to understand our customers' applications, identify suitable solutions, and build lasting business relationships through dependable products and responsive service. Whether serving the footwear, furniture, automotive, construction, packaging, or general manufacturing sectors, we aim to be a reliable partner in our customers' day-to-day operations and long-term growth.

As we continue to develop our capabilities and expand our product portfolio, our focus remains on strengthening local manufacturing, improving product performance, and delivering solutions that businesses can rely on. We believe lasting partnerships are built through consistent quality, practical expertise, and trust earned over time.
