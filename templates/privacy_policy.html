Privacy Policy

How Uniworld Adhesives & Sealants collects, uses, stores, and protects information submitted through our website and quotation channels.

Contents
1. Information We Collect 2. How We Use Business Information 3. Data Sharing & Confidentiality 4. Cookies & Technical Logs 5. Data Security & Retention 6. Contact Our Data Desk

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a quotation request, sample inquiry, or technical support form on our website, Uniworld Adhesives & Sealants may collect business contact details including your name, company or factory name, phone/WhatsApp number, email address, substrate requirements, and order volume preferences.

2. How We Use Business Information

We use the information you provide strictly for legitimate B2B communication and order fulfillment:

  • Preparing accurate factory quotations, bulk pricing, and sample shipments.
  • Providing Technical Data Sheets (TDS), Safety Data Sheets (SDS), and substrate recommendations.
  • Coordinating dispatch and logistics from our Dhaka Head Office or Rupganj manufacturing plant.

3. Data Sharing & Confidentiality

Uniworld Adhesives & Sealants does not sell, rent, or trade your personal or corporate contact details to third parties. Information is shared only with authorized internal sales, R&D, and logistics personnel as needed to fulfill your inquiry, or where required by applicable law in Bangladesh.

4. Cookies & Technical Logs

Our website uses essential session and security cookies (such as CSRF protection tokens) to ensure form submissions work securely, along with standard server access logs to maintain site performance and security.

5. Data Security & Retention

We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect submitted records from unauthorized access. You may request correction or deletion of your contact records at any time by emailing us.
