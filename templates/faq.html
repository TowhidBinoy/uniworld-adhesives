Uniworld manufactures industrial adhesives, primers, surface cleaners, and silicone sealants for footwear & leather goods, woodworking & foam lamination, construction & glazing, retail hardware, and automotive/general assembly across Bangladesh.
Low-surface-energy soles like EVA and Phylon require EVA Primer after cleaning with Synthetic Cleaner so the adhesive wets and anchors properly. Molded PU, PVC, and TPR soles benefit from PU/PVC Primer to treat surface plasticizers and maximize peel strength.
Polyurethane (PU) Adhesive requires heat reactivation (55°C–65°C) and delivers maximum heat resistance and peel strength on PVC, PU, and leather uppers. Graft CR Adhesive is a grafted chloroprene formula offering strong cold or warm contact grab on primed EVA, rubber, and synthetic substrates.
Acetic Silicone Sealant cures fast with a slight vinegar odor and is ideal for glass, ceramic tiles, and sanitary ware. Neutral Cure Silicone Sealant is low-odor and non-corrosive—safe for mirrors, coated metals, marble/stone, and sensitive plastics.
Both provide low-viscosity spray atomization and soft, non-hardening foam seams. SB602 White adds visual spray-coverage contrast on white PU foam and mattress ticking so operators avoid missed spots or over-spraying.
Allow solvents to flash off completely until the film feels dry to the back of a finger yet tacky (typically 3–8 minutes for SBS spray adhesives and 10–20 minutes for brush-grade PU or Synthetic Rubber contact adhesives, depending on factory temperature and humidity). Pressing before solvent flash-off can trap vapor and weaken bond strength.
Use a standard gravity or pressure-feed adhesive spray gun with a 1.5 mm to 2.0 mm nozzle at 3.5–5.0 bar (50–70 PSI) air pressure, holding the gun 20–30 cm from the foam or panel surface for a uniform web pattern without cobwebbing.
Nail Free provides high initial grab within minutes of firm pressing; heavy vertical panels, skirting boards, or decorative trims should be braced or supported for 24 hours while full structural cure completes over 48–72 hours.
