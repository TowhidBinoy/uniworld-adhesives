Who We Are

Uniworld Solutions is a pioneering force in the adhesive and chemical industry, dedicated to developing innovative bonding technologies that redefine reliability and performance. Headquartered in Dhaka, Bangladesh, with a modern production facility in Narayanganj, we serve diverse industrial sectors with customized, high-performance adhesive systems and chemical formulations.

Every product we make reflects research-driven engineering, global manufacturing standards, and a deep understanding of how materials connect and endure.
